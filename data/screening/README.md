# Literature Screening

This directory contains the records associated with the screening and eligibility assessment of the literature identified for the cancer survival prediction review.

## Screening Process

The screening records document the progression of identified publications through the review process.

The screening process includes:

* Duplicate identification
* Title and abstract screening
* Full-text eligibility assessment
* Inclusion and exclusion decisions
* Reasons for exclusion

## Screening Records

The screening datasets maintain the relationship between each study and its screening decision.

Where applicable, reviewer decisions and disagreements are documented as part of the screening record.

## Eligibility

The inclusion and exclusion decisions are based on the eligibility criteria defined in the review protocol.

The screening records provide an auditable link between the initially identified literature and the studies included in the final review.

## Data Management

Screening records are maintained under Git version control.

Changes to screening decisions are recorded through the repository history to preserve transparency in the review process.
